## 👋 About

I’m interested in the small details that make complex work reliable: understanding why an error happened, making the next step clearer, and helping teams apply the same standard consistently.

## 🧭 How I Work

```text
Understand the issue → Make the process clearer → Help the team apply it
```

- **Understand the issue:** Look beyond the immediate error to identify what happened and where the process became unclear.  
- **Make the process clearer:** Use practical checks, documentation, and shared clarification.  
- **Help the team apply it:** Share findings and guidance so colleagues can apply the same standard consistently.
